# WasteNot: Digital Food Waste Reduction Platform

## Project Overview
**WasteNot** is a web-based platform designed to minimize food waste by connecting food providers (restaurants, bakeries, and cafeterias) with consumers and NGOs who can claim, purchase, or receive surplus food donations.  

By promoting redistribution instead of disposal, WasteNot supports the United Nations Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) 12: **Responsible Consumption and Production**.

The system allows:
- Providers to **post surplus food** items for sale or donation.  
- Consumers and NGOs to **browse, request, and confirm pickups** of available food.  
- Admins to **manage platform users, listings, and reports** for transparency and impact tracking.

---

## Problem Statement
Globally, millions of tons of edible food are discarded every year while many communities face food insecurity.  
In Cebu City, organic waste makes up a large portion of solid waste, and local composting facilities remain underutilized.  
**WasteNot** aims to address this by creating a **digital redistribution system** that prevents edible food from reaching landfills and instead brings it to people and organizations who can use it.

---

## Project Objectives
- **Reduce food waste** by redistributing surplus edible food.  
- **Support communities** by making food more affordable and accessible.  
- **Provide analytics** for providers and admins to track environmental and social impact.  
- **Encourage responsible consumption** through transparency and community collaboration.
