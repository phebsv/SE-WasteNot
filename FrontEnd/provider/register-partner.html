Provider Application Process

Due to compliance requirements, all Provider registrations require external application and administrative approval.

1. Complete External Application: If you have not done so, please fill out our official application form to submit your business details and required certifications.

Go to Provider Application Form

2. Await Review: Your application is under review (typically 3-5 business days) to verify business legitimacy and food safety compliance.

3. Receive Credentials: Upon approval, we will send an email with your unique **Account ID** and a **Temporary Password**.

4. First-Time Login: Use the provided credentials on the login page. You will be required to **immediately reset your temporary password** for security.
