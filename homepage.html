How does our app works?

Local restaurants, bakeries, and cafeterias post surplus items that are still perfectly good to eat but would otherwise go to waste.

Shoppers browse affordable surplus food nearby, with filters for location, and expiry date.

Charities and community organizations request available surplus food to distribute to their beneficiaries.

An easy dashboard highlights items nearing expiry, letting providers quickly post them for sale or donation.

Both providers and admins can see how much food was saved, donated, and redistributed—turning waste into measurable change.
